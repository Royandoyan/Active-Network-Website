Active Network Philippines Inc. is establishing a high-quality assisted living facility in Cebu designed to serve international retirees and seniors seeking reliable long-term care.

Our Background

Originating from Osaka, Japan, our organization brings internationally recognized care practices to the Philippines. Japan's rapidly aging population and the world-class skills of Filipino healthcare professionals created the perfect foundation for this initiative.

By welcoming international retirees to Cebu, the company strengthens local senior services while providing foreign residents with structured and professional care in a tropical, peaceful environment.

The Problem We Aim to Solve

Many elderly individuals require structured long-term care, reliable healthcare access, and safe living environments. Active Network Philippines addresses this need by providing organized assisted living services that combine international care standards with compassionate Filipino support.

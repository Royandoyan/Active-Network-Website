Our Services

Senior Care Services Designed for You

Our programs focus on assisted living, nursing care, wellness activities, and recovery support — tailored to each senior's needs.

Long-Term & Short-Term Care Residence

Our assisted living residence provides seniors with secure and comfortable living spaces supported by 24/7 supervision and personalized daily care from trained healthcare professionals.

Day Services

Designed for seniors who live at home but require daytime supervision, assistance with daily activities, and opportunities for social interaction in a supportive and safe environment.

Balikbayan Retirement Services

This program is designed for returning Filipino expatriates (Balikbayans) who wish to retire in the Philippines while receiving structured senior care and wellness support.

Nursing Care Plans

Each resident receives a personalized nursing care plan based on their medical condition, health needs, and level of assistance required for daily living — crafted by our professional nursing staff.

Post-Hospital Recovery Care

Active Network Philippines provides recovery support for seniors who have recently been discharged from hospitals and require rehabilitation, monitoring, and assisted recovery services.

Lifestyle & Wellness Programs

  • Supervised physical exercises and mobility activities
  • Social and community activities to prevent isolation
  • Emotional support and structured daily routines
  • Nutritional planning with Filipino-Japanese wellness-focused meals

Our care team offers a free consultation and will create the right plan for you or your loved one.
